# Verified Wiring and Schematics

Keep the checked circuit schematic (PDF or editable source), pin-to-pin wiring diagram, power-distribution drawing and bill of materials here.

Record component part numbers, sensor supply and output levels, pull-ups, resistor/capacitor values, regulator input/output voltage and current rating, connector pin order, fuse/current limit and ground arrangement. Mark a schematic as verified only after comparing it with the physical build and meter-checking relevant connections. The prototype photographs do not establish a complete circuit topology.
